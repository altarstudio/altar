FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before You Ask

WHERE WILL I RECEIVE MY DIGITAL PRODUCT?

After purchasing, your digital product should be delivered through the method specified on the product page.

CAN I USE ALTAR RESOURCES FOR PERSONAL EDITS?

Yes. ALTAR resources are created for editors to use within their creative editing workflow.

CAN I SHARE OR REDISTRIBUTE A PACK?

No. Purchased digital resources should not be redistributed, reuploaded or shared as your own product.

DO YOU OFFER REFUNDS?

Because ALTAR products are digital, refund availability may depend on the specific situation and purchase terms.
