OUR STORY

More Than
An Effect.

ALTAR was created from the belief that editing is more than simply putting clips together. Every colour, transition and visual decision has the ability to change how a moment feels.

A scene can become warmer, colder, darker, softer or entirely unfamiliar with a single change in atmosphere. ALTAR exists to create resources for those moments.

This is a growing collection of creative tools made for editors who want to build worlds, shape emotion and create something that feels intentional.

THE ALTAR MANIFESTO

Editing is not only about what the audience sees. It is about what they feel.

Every resource within ALTAR is created with atmosphere, movement and visual storytelling in mind.
